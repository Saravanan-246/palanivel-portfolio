StudyFlow

Project
Study priority & academic planning system

Tells you which subject to revise first

Before exams every subject feels urgent, so nothing gets done well. StudyFlow takes each subject's exam date, credit weight, difficulty and current preparation level, scores them into one weighted priority number, and ranks them. The dashboard shows the ranked table, charts the weak areas, and exports the plan as CSV so it can be printed and stuck on a wall.

  • Weighted priority score from exam proximity, credits, difficulty and readiness.
  • Ranked table that reorders live as inputs change.
  • Plotly Express charts for subject-wise comparison.
  • CSV export of the generated study plan.
