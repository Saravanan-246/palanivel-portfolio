Overview

Students typically decide what to study next by instinct. StudyFlow replaces that guesswork with a simple, transparent calculation: subject details entered by the student are combined into a weighted priority score.

Subjects are then ranked by that score, a study recommendation is produced from the ranking, and everything is surfaced in one interactive dashboard — a ranked table, Plotly charts and a CSV export of the result.

Documented features

  • 01 Subject ranking
  • 02 Weighted priority score
  • 03 Study recommendation
  • 04 Interactive dashboard
  • 05 Ranked table
  • 06 Plotly charts
  • 07 CSV export

Process flow

  1. 01 Input Subject details entered in the app.
  2. 02 Weighted priority Inputs combined into a single priority score.
  3. 03 Subject ranking Subjects ordered by priority score.
  4. 04 Study recommendation Guidance derived from the ranking.
  5. 05 Dashboard Ranked table, charts and CSV export.
